The student is overconfident on Loops — they are getting answers wrong that the model expected them to get right. They do not know they are wrong, so they will not go back and repair it. The edge to Arrays now binds tighter than the map says: harder to cross, for this student only.

Help-seeking is executive — asking for answers, not understanding. At Arrays only the bottom rungs are open: diagrams and hints, no code. This is the sentence you already had, and it is one rung of one node.

The dynamic DAG

Same map, same student. Four signals, each acting on a different part of the graph: the nodes, an edge, the horizon, and a rung. Nothing metacognitive opens a node the map keeps closed.

SignalActs onWhat changesLayer
masterynodeswhich topics are openABAC
calibrationan edgehow hard a prerequisite bindsCAC → ABAC
cognitive loadthe horizonhow far ahead is reachableCAC
help-seekinga runghow much is shown at a nodeCAC

Calibration crosses the boundary. Its trigger is metacognitive, but the thing it moves is a credential threshold: the prerequisite bar rises from θ = 0.75 to 0.90 for this student, on this edge. It is the one place the learner model reaches into the authorization system rather than into the wording of a reply. An earlier draft of this page gave that role to rushing (discounting evidence behind a node); that signal was dropped — see below — and calibration carries the claim instead.

Both gaps are properties of the corpus, not of the code — each was proven by simulation rather than assumed. Everything runs in shadow: a full decision is formed and logged on every turn, and no learner is affected until cac_enforce is switched on.

It flagged 91.3% of learners, and halving their BKT evidence would have corrupted mastery, certification and the dashboard on the strength of how fast the backend replied.

Self-report is both starved (6 rows) and declared by the subject of the policy — a learner who notices that admitting confidence tightens their gate stops admitting it. prediction_log.p_bkt_pred is written on every evidence event, is never shown to the learner, and cannot be faked.
complete mediationthe cap applied on the concept paththree of four answering paths were bypassing it entirely — a learner capped at hints could reach full code by asking “why does my pointer code crash?” or “write a program using pointers”. All four paths are now mediated, with a test that fails if a fifth is added.
load thresholdsfixed constants (0.7 / 0.5)calibrated from the population and versioned, so a decision stays replayable after the numbers move. Guarded against poisoning: an attacker shifting the distribution can move the bar only within a band anchored to the first honest measurement.

The invariant is unchanged and is enforced structurally rather than by convention: every signal narrows through one function, which takes a minimum for the rung and a maximum for the prerequisite bar. There is no vocabulary for widening, so a later signal cannot add one without editing that function and noticing what it is doing. Formally, M(C,K,S) ⊆ M(C,K) — adding what is known about the student can only shrink what they may obtain.
