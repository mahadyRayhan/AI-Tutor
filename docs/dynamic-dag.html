The instructor's map

A fixed prerequisite graph. Same for every student. Nothing on it moves.

+ Mastery

BKT colors the nodes

Green is earned, amber is unlocked, grey is locked. This is what ABAC reads: what may be opened.

+ Calibration

The edge tightens

The student is overconfident on Loops — confidence higher than results. The edge to Arrays now binds tighter than the map says. It is harder to cross, for this student only.

+ Cognitive load

The horizon shrinks

Load is high. Reach contracts to one step. Strings and Structures are still on the map, but off the horizon for this session.

+ Help-seeking

The rung is capped

Help-seeking is executive — asking for answers, not understanding. At Arrays only the bottom rungs are open: diagrams and hints, no code. This is the sentence you already had, and it is one rung of one node.

+ Rushing

Evidence flows back

Answers on Loops came faster than the mastery they show. That evidence is discounted, and the green on Loops is now in doubt. Metacognition reaches back into mastery.
