This is exactly the narrative you need for a "V2" research paper or a major product launch. You are shifting the paradigm from **System-Enforced Compliance** (V1) to **Empowered, Self-Regulated Learning** (V2). 

In V1, the system treated the student as a potential adversary who might cheat. In V2, the system treats the student as a partner, empowering them to take control of their own learning journey while still resting safely within the instructor's guardrails.

Here are the concrete, architectural differences between the PDF (V1) and the Current Codebase (V2), structured perfectly to highlight the shift to a **Dual Model (Instructor-Regulated + Student-Regulated Learning)**.

---

### 1. From Static Prerequisites to Goal-Oriented Dynamic Curriculums
*   **V1 (The PDF):** Relied strictly on ABAC (Attribute-Based Access Control). If a student asked about "Arrays", it checked the graph. If they didn't know "Loops", it blocked access and redirected them. It was purely *reactive* and strictly *Instructor-Regulated*.
*   **V2 (Current Code):** Introduces **Hybrid Curriculum Generation**. The student inputs a real-world goal (*"Build a Calculator"*). The system uses the "Pro" LLM to translate that goal into required concepts, then queries the Instructor's Neo4j Graph to map a 5-step path.
*   **Why it matters (SRL Shift):** This bridges Instructor-Regulated Learning (the graph dependencies) with **Student-Regulated Learning (Forethought & Planning)**. Students aren't just blindly following a syllabus; they are pursuing their own passions, and the AI dynamically builds the stepping stones to get them there.

### 2. From Passive Filtering to Proactive Metacognitive Interventions
*   **V1 (The PDF):** The Safety Gatekeeper waited for the student to ask a question, filtered the context, and generated a safe response. If the student never asked a question, the system did nothing.
*   **V2 (Current Code):** The system has **Proactive Agency**. 
    *   **Spaced Repetition (Warm-Ups):** On login, it interrupts the student with a modal asking them to summarize a previously mastered topic. 
    *   **Socratic Withholding:** If the student asks for an answer they already know, the AI pushes back: *"My records show you already mastered this! Look at your notes. How do YOU think we should approach this?"*
    *   **Feynman Challenge:** After passing a quiz, it challenges them to explain it "to a 5-year-old."
*   **Why it matters (SRL Shift):** This actively trains **Self-Reflection**. Instead of cognitive offloading (letting the AI do the thinking), the system forces the student to retrieve information from their own brain, moving knowledge from short-term to long-term memory.

### 3. From Infinite Loops to Frustration Management & Escalation
*   **V1 (The PDF):** If a student couldn't solve a coding problem, they could ask the AI 50 times. The AI would safely provide 50 hints, but the student would remain stuck in a frustration spiral.
*   **V2 (Current Code):** Introduces the **3-Strike Escalation Protocol** and **Emotion Profiling** (via `DistilRoBERTa`). If the student fails a scaffolding step 3 times, or their text registers high anger/frustration (>0.6), the AI halts the loop. It offers them agency: *"You've tried this 3 times. Do you want partial code, or do you want me to draft a message to your TA?"*
*   **Why it matters (SRL Shift):** This teaches healthy **Help-Seeking Behavior** (a core component of performance monitoring). It recognizes that struggling is good, but spiraling is bad, and gives the student the power to choose how to get unstuck.

### 4. From One-Size-Fits-All to Neuro-Inclusive Environmental Control
*   **V1 (The PDF):** Every student received the exact same pedagogical output format (e.g., text + hint).
*   **V2 (Current Code):** Introduces **Total User Environmental Control**. Students have a settings modal where they can enable Dyslexia-friendly fonts, High-Contrast code blocks, 45-minute ADHD break reminders, "Concise Mode" (TL;DR), or "Literal Mode" (stripping away confusing analogies for Autistic learners).
*   **Why it matters (SRL Shift):** A massive part of Self-Regulated Learning is knowing *how* you learn best. By giving the student control over the AI's "System Prompt" and the UI CSS, they regulate their own sensory and cognitive load. The Instructor provides the content; the Student controls the delivery.

### 5. Dual-LLM Architecture for Real-Time Cognition
*   **V1 (The PDF):** Used a single LLM API for everything, balancing safety vs. speed.
*   **V2 (Current Code):** Uses a **Task-Specific Dual-LLM approach**. 
    *   It uses ultra-fast local NLP (`SentenceTransformers`) for instant Intent Classification and Quiz Grading.
    *   It uses `Gemini Flash` for real-time conversational streaming and safety checks.
    *   It uses `Gemini 1.5 Pro` (with Few-Shot Prompting) strictly in the background for complex Curriculum Planning and Edge-Case Code Reviews.
*   **Why it matters (UX):** It solves the "Latency vs. Reasoning" tradeoff. The chat feels instantly responsive (keeping ADHD/impatient learners engaged), but the underlying curriculum logic is mathematically sound and hallucination-free.
